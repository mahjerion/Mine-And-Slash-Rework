CTE2 Compendium

Everything Craft to Exile 2 can roll, drop and craft, read straight out of the pack the server runs, so what is on these pages is what you will actually see in game. Every page is searchable and filterable, and each entry shows its item id underneath its name because the id is what you type into JEI.

__SECTIONS__
NAMESEvery name here is the one the game shows you. Where something has no name of its own, which is mostly enchants and Watcher's Eye modifiers, its internal id is tidied up and used instead, and you can tell because the same text repeats underneath.
WEIGHTWeight is how often something is picked out of its pool, so it only means anything against the other entries in the same table. The bar next to each number is scaled to the heaviest entry in that table.
NOT RANDOMSomething marked not randomly generated exists and works, but never drops on its own. You get it by crafting or upgrading, or it is placed by a specific encounter. Affixes are the exception: there, weight 0 really does mean it can never appear, so those are left out.
MISSINGA handful of entries are marked in the data as deliberately undocumented, mostly older duplicates the pack keeps around beside the live version. Those are not listed.
SOURCEBuilt from the mod's own data with the pack's overrides layered on top, in the order the game loads them, so where the pack retunes something this shows the retuned value.
